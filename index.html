05FAQ

Questions & answers.

Didn’t find an answer? Write to us — the DexTor OS team replies in Telegram and Discord.

Contact us →
Is DexTor OS a wallet, an exchange or an operating system?
It’s a portable, Linux-based operating system. Wallet, DEX, staking and messenger are built into it — no separate apps to install and configure.
Do I need to install anything?
No. The Windows setup wizard downloads and verifies the image and writes it to a USB drive. After that DexTor OS runs from USB — in a separate window or at boot on any PC, with nothing installed on the disk.
What happens if I lose my USB?
The drive is fully encrypted: without the password its data can’t be read. Keep your wallet recovery phrase separately from the USB — with it you can restore access to your funds on a new drive.
How do I know the image is genuine?
The setup wizard checks the developer’s digital signature and the SHA-256 checksum. If anything doesn’t match, writing to the USB won’t start.
Is there mobile support?
Not yet. An iOS and Android mobile wallet is planned in phase 4 of the roadmap.
What is the “Kill Password” feature?
This emergency feature lets you instantly wipe all data from the USB — permanently and securely — in case of physical threat.
