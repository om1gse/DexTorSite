Token, Premium & Ecosystem

Months 13–155 milestones

The DXTR token launches, premium features arrive and the ecosystem opens up to third-party developers.

DEX & AI Expansion

Months 12–166 milestones

The DEX matures with order books and liquidity, while AI starts guarding against phishing and risky contracts.

Web3, Marketplace & Mobile

DexTor goes beyond the desktop: a decentralized marketplace and a mobile wallet for iOS and Android.
